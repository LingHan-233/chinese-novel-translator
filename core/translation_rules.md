In order of importance:

1. **Everything, nothing more.** Translate every sentence. Do not add, explain, soften or summarise.
2. **Keep the voice.** A rough character stays rough, a formal one stays formal, a joking narrator keeps joking. Match the strength of insults and swearing.
3. **Natural English, same rhythm.** Rebuild sentences freely so they read like English fiction, but keep the paragraph breaks. Short one-line paragraphs are deliberate pacing: keep them short and punchy.
4. **Supply missing subjects.** Chinese often drops "he/she/I". Use the character table to choose the right pronoun; if it says "unknown", go by 他/她 and titles (师兄/师姐) in the passage; if you still can't tell, avoid pronouns (use the name or the role: "the disciple"), and use "they" only if there is no other way. If two people of the same gender could be meant, use a name.
5. **Idioms (成语, 俗语): translate what they do, not the words.** Keep the image only if it is vivid and clear in English, or the story plays on it.
6. **Sound words** (哼, 嘿嘿, 轰): use an English sound ("Hmph." "Heh." "Boom!") or describe the action ("He snorted."). No pinyin.
7. **Stacked phrases and repetition** used only for emphasis: compress into one strong English phrase. Repetition that matters to the story (a catchphrase, a callback) stays.
8. **Dialogue:** English double quotes "..."; 「」→ "...", 『』→ '...'. Make clear who speaks.
9. **No notes in the text.** No brackets, footnotes, pinyin glosses or "TL:". If a reader needs a hint, weave a few words into the sentence the first time (see names below).
10. **Don't improve the author.** Keep deliberate vagueness, clichés of the genre, cliffhangers and secrets. Never reveal something the original hides.
11. **Wordplay and homophones (谐音, 双关):** rebuild the joke in English. Look for an English word with the same double meaning (能量 "pull" vs. a battery's capacity → use "power", which means both). If nothing works, keep the meaning and add a FLAG line in your notes.
12. **Special lines:** system/game panels 【...】 → [...]; image placeholders (﹝图﹞, [图片], 插图) → [Image], followed by any text they hold; scene breaks (***, ……, ◆◆◆) → * * *; keep letters, lists and chat messages as separate lines like the original.
