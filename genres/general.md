<!-- Genre module for anything that is not xianxia or urban. Included in task files when genre = general. -->
- Forms of address: 哥/姐 between friends → the name, or "bro"/"sis" only if the tone is that casual. 叔叔/阿姨 said by a child or young person to an adult → "Uncle"/"Auntie" or "sir"/"ma'am" — pick one per character and keep it.
- Family titles (大伯, 二舅, 表哥...) → "Uncle", "cousin" + name when it matters; English does not need the exact branch of the family unless the story does.
- Money stays in yuan (一万块 → ten thousand yuan). Don't convert to dollars.
- 您 vs 你: show politeness through word choice ("sir", "please", fuller sentences), not by explaining.
- Period settings (historical, palace): keep it readable modern English with a slightly formal tone; avoid fake old English (thee, thou, 'tis).
