- Forms of address: 哥/姐 between friends → the name, or 兄貴/姉さん only if the tone is that casual. 叔叔/阿姨 said by a young person to an adult → おじさん/おばさん. Pick one per character and keep it.
- Family titles (大伯, 二舅, 表哥...) → 伯父さん, 叔父さん, 従兄 + name when it matters; Japanese does not need the exact branch of the family unless the story does.
- Money stays in yuan (一万块 → 一万元). Don't convert to yen.
- 您 vs 你: show politeness with Japanese speech levels (です・ます, 敬語), not by explaining.
- Period settings (historical, palace): readable Japanese with a slightly formal tone (時代小説 style is fine); don't overdo archaic words.
