## [general] Short paragraphs, dropped subject, small talk
ZH:
[[P1]] 门开了。
[[P2]] 是她。
[[P3]] 沈默站在门口，手里的伞还在滴水。看见屋里的人，愣了一下，又像什么都没发生似的，把伞靠在墙边。
[[P4]] "吃了吗？"
EN:
[[P1]] The door opened.
[[P2]] It was her.
[[P3]] Shen Mo stood in the doorway, rain still dripping from the umbrella in his hand. At the sight of who was inside, he froze — then, as if nothing had happened, leaned the umbrella against the wall.
[[P4]] "Have you eaten?"
Why: one-line paragraphs stay one line; the missing subject is supplied; the awkward everyday greeting is kept because the awkwardness is the point.

## [general] Idioms: keep the function, drop an image English readers won't get
ZH:
[[P1]] 他把这桩买卖说得天花乱坠，王经理却只是笑了笑，心里早就有了数。
[[P2]] 最后那句补充，简直是画蛇添足。
EN:
[[P1]] He made the deal sound like a gift from heaven. Manager Wang only smiled; he had sized the man up long ago.
[[P2]] That last remark only spoiled it.
Why: 天花乱坠 and 画蛇添足 are translated by what they mean in the scene, not word by word.

## [xianxia] Forms of address, realms, tone without "said faintly"
ZH:
[[P1]] "师兄，"苏晴压低了声音，"那位前辈……似乎已是金丹境。"
[[P2]] 林凡没有回头，只是淡淡道："金丹又如何？"
EN:
[[P1]] "Senior Brother," Su Qing whispered, "that senior... I think he's already at the Core Formation realm."
[[P2]] Lin Fan didn't turn around. "So what if he is?"
Why: titles follow the genre convention; 淡淡道 becomes no tag at all — the calm is in the line.

## [xianxia] Arrogant self-reference and insults
ZH:
[[P1]] "本座活了三千年，还轮不到你一个筑基小辈来教训！"
EN:
[[P1]] "I have lived for three thousand years. I will not be lectured by some Foundation Establishment whelp!"
Why: 本座 becomes "I" with an imperious tone; 小辈 keeps its contempt as "whelp" instead of a neutral "junior".

## [urban] System panels and money
ZH:
[[P1]] 【叮！检测到宿主遭受羞辱，打脸系统已激活。】
[[P2]] 【新手任务：让对方当众道歉。奖励：现金十万元。】
[[P3]] 陈阳愣住了。十万？他兜里现在只剩二十三块五。
EN:
[[P1]] [Ding! Humiliation detected. Face-Slapping System activated, Host.]
[[P2]] [Beginner Quest: Make the other party apologize in public. Reward: 100,000 yuan in cash.]
[[P3]] Chen Yang froze. A hundred thousand? He had exactly twenty-three yuan fifty to his name.
Why: panels stay short and game-like in square brackets; money stays in yuan; the joke in P3 lands on the exact small number.

## [historical] Forms of address and rank
ZH:
[[P1]] "老爷，茶凉了，奴给您换一盏。"
[[P2]] "叫什么老爷？"方知县眼皮都没抬，"本官今年才二十三。"
EN:
[[P1]] "Master, your tea has gone cold. Let me bring you a fresh cup."
[[P2]] "Don't 'Master' me." Magistrate Fang didn't even lift his eyelids. "This official is twenty-three years old."
Why: the servant's humility (奴) is in the wording, not a literal "this slave"; the correction of the form of address survives in English; 本官 becomes a pompous "this official" because the pomposity is the joke.
